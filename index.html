Overview

An interactive audio challenge built around practical digital signal processing. Teams record short voice clips, which get scrambled, pitch-shifted, and format-altered live using signal-manipulation algorithms — then race to identify their own teammates' voices in a blind test.

02

Rounds

The Scramble — Elimination

Each member records a 5-second standardized phrase. Our Python system applies pitch scaling and formant shifting, then scrambles the playback order. Teams have to match each altered voice snippet back to the correct teammate.

Format: audio-based blind identification Scoring: speed + accuracy Advance: top 50%

The Deep-fake Duel — Finals

Finalist teams receive a mix of genuine samples, heavily filtered voices, and fully synthesized clips. Teams analyse both the audio and live on-screen spectrograms to classify each one as real or modified.

Format: DSP vs. AI voice discrimination Scoring: highest cumulative score wins
