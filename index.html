Event Rounds

ROUND 01

The Scramble

A pure test of auditory memory. The system scrambles your teammate's playback array and heavily distorts the frequency. Teams compete in a fast-paced blind test to correctly identify who is speaking under the distortion.

ELIMINATION: Top 50% advance based on speed/accuracy.
ROUND 02

The Deep-fake Duel

Finalists face a gauntlet: genuine human audio vs. completely synthetic/altered variations. Monitor live on-screen spectrogram readouts to spot visual anomalies and classify the audio.

FINALS: Highest cumulative score across identification wins.
