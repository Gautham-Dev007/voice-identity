Questions?

Here's what people usually ask before signing up.

Do I need any prior experience?

No. Voice Identity is open to all engineering branches and all years — first-years included, no technical background required. Curiosity and a working pair of ears is the whole entry bar.

How big should my team be?

3 to 4 members. The same team plays through both rounds — The Scramble and The Deep-fake Duel — so pick people you'll want beside you when it gets down to the wire.

What happens right after I register?

The event WhatsApp group link appears immediately after you submit the registration form — that's where all logistics, reminders, and last-minute updates get shared.

When do registrations close?

2:20 PM IST on 22 September 2026 — the same day and time the event itself kicks off at LHC Seminar Hall, MSRIT. Don't wait until the window's about to shut.

Does filling out the SPS interest form commit me to anything?

No — it's separate from event registration and doesn't obligate you to join anything. It just puts you on a shorter list to hear from us first, whenever SPS recruitment opens later this semester.
